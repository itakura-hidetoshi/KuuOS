    1.1 Finite four-cell comparison chains with ORIGINAL source vertices¶

    Fix the genuine original F54 dependent long/short route endpoints, including both Nat depth indices, original Blocks and two Site categories. An indexed NativeGK5ASSourceVertexChain f g connects two original F55 Type-valued 3-proof terms f and g.

    Each step has another original F55 proof h as a source vertex and an actual selected Type-valued P94A 4-cell

    w : P94A(φ f, φ h),

    where φ is the constructor-recursive original F55→P92B structural map proven in GK5AI. This is not a numerical C60 shadow, Eq-only observation, or inferred original source cell.

    toSelected composes all such source-vertex steps vertically into a true selected P94A four-cell between the structural images of the chain endpoints.

    A HasBadStep witness is a local selected P94A comparison between consecutive original F55 proofs for which no genuine original P89 Type-valued 4-cell comparison exists.

    The central GK5AS constructor-induction dichotomy is:

    For every source-vertex comparison chain(f,g):
  Nonempty(P89FourCell(f,g)) OR HasBadStep(chain).

    In particular:

    • If ¬ HasBadStep(chain), the full chain reflects to an original P89 4-cell at its endpoints.
    • If original BadPair(f,g) holds, then every provided source-vertex chain f→g has at least one bad local step. This is conditional on such a chain having been supplied; no assertion that every selected P94A witness admits such a decomposition is made.
    • Conversely, a bad local step supplies a genuine GK5AQ nonreflection bad pair among original F55 terms.

    The theorem nativeGK5ASAllSourceVertexChains_noBad_iff_injective makes this an exact criterion for injectivity of the original P89→selected P94A four-class quotient: it is injective iff no source-vertex chain of any length contains a bad step. The singleton-chain case recovers GK5AQ's reflection condition. This does NOT assert either side holds unconditionally.

    1.2 Why selected intermediate P92B histories remain an obstruction¶

    A source-vertex chain is an outer decomposition with original F55 proof terms at its marked intermediate vertices. A single selected P94A four-cell edge can still contain internally non-source P92B evidence, and its existence does not provide an original P89 4-cell. No "all P94A witnesses normalize into primitive source-saturated chains" theorem is claimed.

    This distinction prevents circularly assuming the missing reflection certificate when investigating the original P89→P94A bad-pair boundary.

    1.3 A concrete zero-grade but nonrecoverable north C60 witness¶

    For the REAL three adjacent north C60 F45 stages, selected P92B has an adjoined, genuine assocNaturality 3-cell. Original F55 has no corresponding Type-valued 3-proof term.

    Nevertheless, P94A has the selected reflexive 4-cell:

    refl : P94A(assocNaturality, assocNaturality).

    GK5AR's exact two-component context-unit count parity law proves that every P94A loop 4-cell at this selected 3-proof endpoint has grade:

    (N_L(w) mod 2, N_R(w) mod 2) = (0, 0).

    The theorem nativeGK5ASNorthZeroGrade_not_sourceRecovery combines (1) the existence of a selected 4-cell; (2) the zero grade for every selected loop 4-cell at that endpoint; and (3) the nonexistence of an original F55 source preimage.

    Therefore zero context-unit grade is not a sufficient criterion for descent or original evidence recovery. Since the selected 3-proof is not in the original φ image, this is not a counterexample to the original P89→P94A class map's injectivity: GK5AQ bad pairs require two genuine original source terms.
