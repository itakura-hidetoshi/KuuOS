    F112: independent Kuu migration ledger (2026-10-17 JST)¶

    Authority and meaning¶

    Old 19-stage F112 theorem history (v5.273 through v5.291) is preserved in legacy/v5.291/formal/KUOS/. Those theorems involve old source definitions, including proof-relevant F42/F54/F55, F47, F51/F77, signed original F68 curvature and F69 incidence. Compiling the frozen history does not prove the same statements in the new independent Kuu.* mathematics.

    New current code must use boundary-typed generation and declared congruences, not an old Gray evaluation kernel, a surrogate Bool site or an asserted exchange/naturality axiom.

    Current theorem-bearing units¶

    formal/Kuu/F112/Physical.lean constructs the actual truncated-icosahedral incidence geometry using an explicit original icosahedron (12 vertices, 30 edges, 20 triangular faces), endpoint darts (60), 30 cross plus 60 around-vertex edges (90), and 12 pentagon plus 20 hexagon polygon sites (32). The ordered around-vertex darts define the original triangular corners. The actual derived vertex-edge-face incidence partial order and 360 composable physical flags are constructed with decidable finite proofs. This is neither an invented square surface nor an arbitrary 180/182-element proxy. It supplies geometry only; historical F47 source functors and physical curvature are not smuggled in.

    formal/Kuu/F112/TotalChange.lean proves actual Cat-valued diagram source-change transport, unit/composition, exact index preservation, strict Cat isomorphism and total equivalence, plus quotient natural-isomorphism descent for separately supplied observers respecting Kuu's declared relations. These are mathematical mechanisms shared with former F112 parts 16–17. Input diagrams and old proof-related source change remain to be constructed and proved, so this is not an old theorem by renaming.

    Certified independent polygon higher-source carrier (F112-A through D3)¶

    The current formal/Kuu/F112/ layers all enter the standard KuuOSStrict and KuuOSFormal Lean roots, WITHOUT importing frozen KUOS.*, Gray, F51 or F77.

    • FaceRewrite.lean (F112-A): genuine C60 vertex-edge-polygon triangle two-cell source as a Type-valued proof tree with inverses, vertical composition, both contexts and proven localization/kernel soundness. Raw witnesses remain distinct from unit-prefixed ones.
    • PolygonBoundary.lean (F112-B): each of the 12 true pentagons has five cyclically closed real edges and each of the 20 true hexagons has six. Each edge is incident to the actual polygon and joins its two real darts; physical face-side labels are checked.
    • PolygonRawLoop.lean (F112-C): the dependent finite-prefix construction glues one genuine closed raw zigzag per actual face, with 10 / 12 noncancelled original incidence legs, proven different from raw nil.
    • PolygonFan.lean (F112-D1): each physical boundary edge span dart -> edge <- dart has a genuinely typed Type-valued sector comparison to dart -> actual face <- dart. The comparison is proved sound in the actual Gabriel-Zisman localization and exact kernel quotient, not postulated as a charge/Gray evaluation.
    • PolygonDisk.lean (F112-D2): exact source-level common-face spokes and the generalized telescoping proof give polygonRawLoop_localized_id and polygonRawLoop_kernel_id for all 32 original polygons. The new PolygonDiskRewrite is a Type-valued face-disk generator with separately proved localization/kernel soundness. This does NOT imply raw-loop equality to nil or any historical F68/F77 obstruction vanishing.
    • PolygonSource.lean (F112-D3): PolygonSourcePresentation p independently records all 5/6 raw proof-relevant fan sector witnesses PLUS the certified face-disk term. Canonical and unit-prefixed presentations are provably unequal for every real face. originalEdge_polygonBoundary_iff establishes the precise TWO physical neighboring polygons of each of all 90 edges; there are precisely 180 = 12 * 5 + 20 * 6 = 2 * 90 original boundary-side occurrences. A canonical atlas on all 32 original polygon sites is defined.

    Authority limit: these new types are genuinely independent, but their historical F42 source equality/equivalence has not been shown; neither has the original F54 full rotation syntax, F55 3-cell source, F47 typed mixed boundary comparison or Beck-Chevalley NatIso, or the signed F68/F77 obstructions. A disk loop becomes identity ONLY after a particular localization/kernel quotient; its raw proof witness and possible enriched historical charge must not be erased.
