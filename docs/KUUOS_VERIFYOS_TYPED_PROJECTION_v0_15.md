# VerifyOS typed receipt projection v0.15 — research candidate

This append-only extension instantiates the actual v0.14 Supported, Honors, and Forced definitions and the forced_iff_supported theorem for the closed v0.3 claim vocabulary. The Lean model has 18 claim identifiers, 13 receipt-observable fields, and five unsupported authority/freshness claims. A typed view of a separately admitted receipt records its bound context and the exact field values. The executable projection checks status strings, zero exit codes, natural counts, and the 64-character lowercase hexadecimal digest matching the expected tip. A wrong context, missing field, invalid value, unadmitted receipt, or unsupported field yields no observation. Required identifiers are retained in the output rows.

The candidate establishes value-level projection equivalence with a relational source-validity specification, contextual support and entailment equivalence by applying v0.14, and necessary/sufficient coverage of the required list. An unsupported required claim prevents full observation; snapshot-tip observation does not certify current-tip freshness. The semantic target here is only the value of the supplied typed, admitted receipt. Admission itself, cryptographic authenticity, Python parsing, JSON field extraction, real-world truth, mathematical proof authority and action authority are not proved by this model.

The checker pins exact v0.14 Lean and audit sources, the v0.3 projection, v0.4 registry, v0.13 audit and its fixture. It obtains one actual synthetic v0.13 signed-pair result using the prior test helper, extracts the raw typed fields, and requires the v0.3 runtime projections to equal the signed rows. It builds additional value-level mutation cases (not admitted execution bundles) and two preflight-negative cases, and generates concrete Lean theorems with kernel-checked decide, not native_decide. The tests compare all 18 projected claim values, required identifiers and completeness results against finite expected cases. They do not certify arbitrary Python inputs or the entire publication pipeline. The generated Lean evidence is based on synthetic fixtures only; no private ledger, key or actual user question is committed.

Direct Lean checks the unchanged v0.14 module, v0.15 module, and generated fixture module under the pinned core toolchain. All named declarations are audited for permitted standard axioms. Actual pinned Lean MCP audits the new general theorems. A separate runner can begin only after sandbox success, and must regenerate the same fixture bytes, re-elaborate all modules, and reconcile the direct and raw MCP receipts. These are acceptance conditions, not claimed results. The proof remains a research probe outside the public formal roots pending separate adoption. The existing modules are unchanged.
